Forklift Licence Test Prep AU Privacy Policy

Effective: 1 October 2026 · Last updated: 1 October 2026

Forklift Licence Test Prep AU is an independent study tool. It is not affiliated with Safe Work Australia, WorkSafe, any state or territory regulator, an RTO, or the official National Assessment Instrument.

1. Who We Are

Forklift Licence Test Prep AU is provided by JSM GLOBAL PTY LTD. Questions about this policy can be sent to support@jsmglobal.xyz.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The app is designed to work offline. It may store the following locally on your device:

  • Practice progress and answered-question history
  • Wrong-answer review items and bookmarks
  • Sequential-practice position
  • Mock-exam results and app preferences
  • A cached indication that full access was unlocked

This local study data is not uploaded to JSM Global servers by the app.

3. Crash Reports and Question Feedback

If Sentry is configured for a release, the app may send technical error reports such as app version, operating-system information, stack traces, and limited diagnostic context. We configure Sentry not to send default personally identifying information.

When you report a question or answer, the report may include the question ID, module, selected answer, answer key, and report category. This is used to review content quality and resolve defects. Do not include sensitive personal information in feedback.

4. Purchases

Full access is a one-off non-consumable purchase processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store through RevenueCat. JSM Global does not receive your full payment-card details. Store accounts, receipts, tax records, and payment processing are governed by the relevant store and RevenueCat privacy terms.

5. App Reviews and External Links

The app may request an operating-system app review after a positive study milestone. It does not submit a review without your action. The app may also open external legal, support, or store links. Those destinations have their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Children

The app is intended for people preparing for workplace licensing and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security and Retention

Local study data is controlled by your device and may be removed by clearing app data or uninstalling the app. Technical reports, if enabled, are retained by the relevant monitoring provider according to its policies. No security method is completely risk-free.

8. Your Choices and Requests

You can remove local app data through the device settings. For questions about data handled by JSM Global, contact support@jsmglobal.xyz. We will handle requests in accordance with applicable Australian privacy law.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when the app, legal requirements, or service providers change. The current version will remain available at this page.
